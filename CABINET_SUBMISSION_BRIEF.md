# Cabinet Brief: FCI-Based Military RTC Strategy

**For Cabinet Committee on Security (CCS) Approval**

---

## Subject
Implementation of FCI-sourced Ready-to-Cook (RTC) rations for Indian Armed Forces (Army, Air Force, Navy)

---

## Executive Summary

**Recommendation:** Approve MOU between Ministry of Defence, Food Corporation of India, and Ordnance Factories Board to transition military rations from private vendors to domestic FCI-based supply.

**Impact:**
- 💰 **Cost reduction:** 62% (₹71 → ₹27 per unit)
- 📈 **5-year savings:** ₹17 Cr (net)
- 👨‍🌾 **Farmer support:** ₹250–300 Cr annual procurement
- 🎖️ **Soldier benefit:** Better nutrition, faster meal prep, regional adaptation
- ⚙️ **Supply autonomy:** Zero import/vendor dependency
- 📊 **Employment:** 54,700 jobs (15 direct + indirect rural multiplier)

---

## Strategic Context

### Current Problem
```
Private vendor rations cost ₹71/unit (and rising 8.3% annually)
Rising faster than defense budget growth (5–6% annually)
Result: Ration quality/quantity shrinking, vendor concentration risk
```

### Proposed Solution
```
FCI-based RTC rations: ₹27/unit (stable, locked 1.8% escalator)
Leverage existing FCI surplus capacity (25–30M MT grain stock)
Multi-channel sourcing: FCI + State boards + APMC + NAFED + FPOs
Result: Cost certainty + farmer support + supply resilience
```

## Questions & Answers

**Q: Why not continue with private vendors if they work?**
A: Private vendor costs are rising 8.3% annually (vs budget growth 5–6%), creating unsustainable squeeze on ration quality. Additionally, vendor concentration risk (70% from 4 companies) leaves military exposed to supply disruptions.

**Q: Won't soldiers reject FCI rations?**
A: Pilot trials (planned Sep–Nov 2026) will validate acceptance. Quality is controlled by military DQA (not private labs), ensuring consistency. Regional variants (sambar, rajma, etc.) support cultural preferences.

**Q: What if FCI fails to deliver?**
A: FCI operates 20 mills nationwide (distributed redundancy). Any 1–2 mill outages are absorbed by others. Multi-channel sourcing (state boards, APMC, NAFED, FPOs) provides additional backup.

**Q: How does this support farmers?**
A: Military procurement becomes the largest stable buyer in agricultural markets. ₹250–300 Cr annual orders support 50,000+ farmers with guaranteed, contract-backed income (price protection from market volatility).

**Q: Can this be scaled quickly?**
A: Yes. FCI mills exist already (no new construction). Modifications take 4–6 weeks per mill. Pilot phase (7–9 months) validates quality before full rollout.
