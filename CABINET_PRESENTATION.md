## SLIDE 1: The Problem - Private Vendor Model

### Current Status (Unsustainable)
- **Cost Crisis:** Ration prices rising **8.3% annually** (vs Defense budget growth: 5-6%)
- **Vendor Concentration:** **70% sourced** from just 4 companies (bottleneck risk)
- **Quality Issues:** **±25% variance** between batches; **12-15% soldier rejection** rate
- **Strategic Vulnerability:** **15% re-imported spices** (geopolitical supply shock risk)

### Impact on Defense
- Ration quality/quantity shrinking despite budget allocation
- No flexibility for emergency scaling (60-90 day lead time)
- Price volatility unpredictable (±8-12% annually)
